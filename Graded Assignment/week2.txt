The following procedure finds the highest marks in Mathematics scored by a “male” student
and a “female” student from the “Scores” dataset. But the programmer may have made
mistakes in one or more steps. Identify all such steps (if any). It is a Multiple Select Question
(MSQ).
      Let us read the given procedure step by step.
      Step 1. Arrange all cards in a single pile called Pile 1
      Step 2. Maintain two variables Mmax, Fmax and initialize them to 0
      Step 3. If Pile 1 is empty then stop the iteration
      Step 4. Read the top card in Pile 1
      Step 5. If Gender is “M” and Mmax > Mathematics marks then store Mathematics marks in Mmax
Step 5 does not look right, there is some mistake in it but let us continue because step 6 is
also similar to this one.
      Step 6. If Gender is “F” and Fmax > Mathematics marks then store Mathematics marks in Fmax
      Step 7. Move the current card to another pile called Pile 2 and repeat from step 3
As I said earlier, steps 5 and 6 are similar and there is an identical mistake in both those steps.
Here, we are trying to find gender wise toppers of Mathematics and as we know, while finding
max the conditions should be, Mmax < Mathematics marks and Fmax < Mathematics marks in steps
5 and 6 respectively. Hence, the correct answer of this question is step 5 and 6.


What will variables A and B represent after execution of the following procedure on the
“Paragraph Words” dataset?
      Step 1. Arrange all cards in a single pile called Pile 1
      Step 2. Maintain two variables A, B and initialize them to 0
      Step 3. If Pile 1 is empty then stop the iteration
      Step 4. Read the top card in Pile 1
      Step 5. If Letter count > A then store A in B and Letter count in A
      Step 6. If Letter count < A and Letter count > B then store Letter count in B
      Step 7. Move the current card to another pile called Pile 2 and repeat from step 3
In this question, steps 5 and 6 are responsible for local operations in the given iteration. Step
5 checks the letter count of the current card and if letter count is greater than A then variable
B’s value will get replaced by A. At the same time, step 5 will also store the letter count in
variable A which will be the max letter count in the dataset. This implies that the value of
variable A is copied to variable B before updating the value of variable A. In step 6, letter
count is getting stored in variable B if the current letter count is smaller that variable A but
higher that variable B. Based on these steps, we can conclude that the variable B is storing the
second max letter count in the dataset. Hence, the correct answer of this question is A = Largest
word length and B = Second largest word length.


What will the value of A represent at the end of execution of the given procedure on the

      In this question’s step 5, we are adding letter count to variable B without checking any

after entering a clinic. First patient will arrive at the clinic then the clinic’s admin department
will check if the patient is registered patient or not. If it is a new patient then he/she will have
